The Careoperative

On journeys of sustainability, rooted in care.

We are a collective of mid-career social scientists based at European institutions, working at the intersection of sustainability transformations and leadership. Founded in 2019 through the Postdoc Academy for Transformational Leadership (Robert Bosch Stiftung), we came together not only to produce research, but to practice a different way of doing it — one that takes seriously what it means to work with care.

Four values orient everything we do: care, reflexivity, inclusivity, and collectivity. These are not aspirations pinned to a wall. They are the commitments we return to when the work gets difficult and the world asks more of us than our institutions are designed to give.
