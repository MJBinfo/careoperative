How we are organised

The Careoperative has always understood participation as something that ebbs and flows. Life changes, research pressures shift, and the energy any one of us can bring to collective work moves with the seasons of a career. Rather than treating this as a problem to be managed, we built the collective to accommodate it. Members are either active — regularly engaged in meetings, writing projects, and shared work — or sleeping, stepping back without stepping out.

Sleeping membership is not an ending. It preserves belonging, keeps doors open, and honours the truth that the conditions for full participation are not always within a person's control. When circumstances change, members move back into active engagement. The distinction is practical, not hierarchical: it reflects where someone is at a given moment, not the value of what they have contributed.
